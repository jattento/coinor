# Conan Code 0.6.15

Stops a single conversation from sizing the whole conversation pane. This
is the fix for the misalignment that reproduced specifically after opening
several chats in a row.

## Root cause

`RuntimeHostView` keeps every loaded conversation mounted in a `ZStack`,
and `ConversationTabbedView` does the same for a conversation's tabs.
Neither the stacks nor their children were pinned to the available space.

A `ZStack` takes the size of its largest child and centers all of them. So
one child asking for more room than the detail column has — and
`TerminalSurfaceRepresentable.sizeThatFits` reported an ideal of 900x600
whenever SwiftUI asked with unspecified dimensions — grew the stack past
the column and centered it there. Centering is why the overflow was
symmetric: the left side disappeared under the sidebar, the right side ran
past the window edge, and the tab strip (a plain SwiftUI view inside the
same stack) went with it.

The trigger reported by the user matches exactly: it showed up after
entering several chats. Each conversation adds another child to that
`ZStack`, so every additional chat is another chance for one of them to
inflate it.

## Fix

- `RuntimeHostView`: every runtime child is pinned with
  `.frame(maxWidth: .infinity, maxHeight: .infinity)`, and the stack itself
  is pinned and `.clipped()`.
- `ConversationTabbedView`: the tab `ZStack` and the whole view are pinned
  and clipped the same way.
- `ConversationPaneView`: the split row is pinned to the reader's exact
  size with `.topLeading` alignment and clipped, so a pane cannot drift.
- `TerminalSurfaceRepresentable.sizeThatFits` no longer reports an ideal
  size. It accepts whatever is proposed and demands nothing, so it can
  never size an enclosing stack.

## Validation

- Reproduced the user's trigger against the fix: 14 conversations opened in
  sequence; then 10 back, 8 forward, and 6 more at a 1000pt window width;
  then five rounds interleaving chat switches with Activity Stack toggles,
  a resize down to 860pt, and back to full width. The sidebar, tab strip,
  and terminal stayed correctly positioned in every check, with nothing
  under the sidebar or past the window edge.
- `scripts/dev/run-tests.sh`: 563 tests in 45 suites. Only the documented
  load-sensitive `oversizedOutputIsTruncatedThroughTheGitRunner` timing
  flake failed under full-suite load; it passes in isolation and its files
  are untouched here.
- Arm64 Release build, `scripts/release/verify-app.sh`, `git diff --check`,
  and the security gate.
